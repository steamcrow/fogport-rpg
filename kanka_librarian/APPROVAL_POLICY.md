# Fogport Kanka approval policy

## Player-facing material

Public descriptions, public posts, and other player-visible canon require Daniel's approval of the exact proposed content before publication.

## GM/private material

Daniel has granted standing approval for Fogport material that the Librarian classifies and publishes as Kanka private/GM-only content.

Private content:

- does not need to be displayed for approval;
- must remain marked private in Kanka;
- must still be included in the proposal digest and publication receipt;
- must be summarized only by operation count in user-facing reports;
- must never be silently converted to public content;
- must never overwrite public canon merely because it conflicts with a private possibility or plan.

Any uncertainty about whether material is public or private defaults to public-review-required.
